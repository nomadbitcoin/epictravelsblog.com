# Antarctica on a Budget — Yes, It's Possible

An Antarctica cruise doesn't have to cost $15,000. With the right strategy, you can experience the white continent for under $6,000.

## Last-Minute Deals from Ushuaia

The biggest secret in Antarctica travel: **last-minute bookings from Ushuaia, Argentina**. Expedition companies would rather sell cabins at a discount than sail with empty rooms.

### How It Works

1. Fly to Ushuaia 2-3 weeks before you want to sail
2. Visit local travel agencies on Avenida San Martín
3. Negotiate directly — discounts of 30-60% are common
4. Be flexible on dates and cabin type

## Best Time to Book

- **November**: Season opener, often cheapest
- **December-January**: Peak season, highest prices
- **February-March**: Late season deals return

## What's Included

Most expedition cruises include:
- All meals and drinks on board
- Zodiac excursions to shore
- Expert naturalist guides
- Waterproof boots (on loan)

## Companies to Watch

Look for smaller expedition ships (under 200 passengers). They offer more landing opportunities and a more intimate experience.

## Is It Worth It?

Absolutely. Antarctica is unlike anywhere else on Earth. Penguins, icebergs, and complete silence — it changes your perspective on everything.
